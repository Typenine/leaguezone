About these statistics

Historical seasons 2023–2025 and the 2026 season-to-date snapshot use weekly offensive player data from NityaGehlot/nfl-data. Fantasy points are based on the source's PPR totals with receptions adjusted for half-PPR and standard scoring. This is a historical snapshot, not live play-by-play. DEF/DST, custom league scoring, and verified rookie-class filters are not yet supported. Statistics may be revised as sources are improved.
