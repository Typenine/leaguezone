About these statistics

Player and team statistics come directly from nflverse, under CC BY 4.0, subject to underlying data rights. Fantasy points use nflverse PPR values; half-PPR and standard adjust receptions. Kicker scoring uses 3/4/5 points by field-goal distance and 1 per made PAT. Defense/ST points are calculated using sacks, turnovers, touchdowns, safeties, blocked kicks, and game scoreboard points allowed. DST scoring varies by provider, and opponent defensive scores may affect the points-allowed calculation. Custom league scoring, snap counts, and verified rookie filters are not currently available. Data is updated after completed NFL weeks and may change following stat corrections.
