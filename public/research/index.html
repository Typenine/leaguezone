How these statistics are calculated
Per game (FP/G, Tgt/G, Rec/G, Car/G, Touch/G)
Season total divided by recorded games. Bye weeks and missed games are not counted.
L3 / L5 avg
Average fantasy points over the player’s last 3 or 5 recorded games. Shown as missing until the player has that many games.
Wk SD and volatility
Standard deviation of weekly fantasy points across recorded games (minimum 3). Volatility is that deviation divided by the average. Lower is steadier.
High / Low
Best and worst single recorded game this season.
Efficiency
Yards per carry = rushing yards ÷ carries. Yards per target = receiving yards ÷ targets. Yards per reception = receiving yards ÷ receptions. Catch % = receptions ÷ targets. Touches = carries + receptions. A zero denominator is shown as missing (—), not zero.
Qualifying volume
When sorting by an efficiency rate, players below the qualifying volume shown above the table are listed after qualified players.

About these statistics

Player and team statistics come directly from nflverse, under CC BY 4.0, subject to underlying data rights. Fantasy points use nflverse PPR values; half-PPR and standard adjust receptions. Kicker scoring uses 3/4/5 points by field-goal distance and 1 per made PAT. Defense/ST points are calculated using sacks, turnovers, touchdowns, safeties, blocked kicks, and game scoreboard points allowed. DST scoring varies by provider, and opponent defensive scores may affect the points-allowed calculation. Rookie seasons are sourced from historical nflverse player metadata when available, excluding unverifiable records from rookie-only results. Custom league scoring, snap counts, snap share, routes, target share, air yards and expected fantasy points are not available in this data and are not shown. Data may change following stat corrections. DST point totals are estimates, not official Sleeper or ESPN scoring.
